Welcome

Together We Protect Nepal's Green Heritage

Forest Association Nepal Lumbini is committed to conserving forests, protecting biodiversity, promoting sustainable natural resource management, and empowering local communities through awareness, education, and collaboration.

Through partnerships with government agencies, community forest groups, educational institutions, and environmental organizations, we continue working towards a greener and healthier future for generations to come.

About Us

Who We Are

We believe healthy forests are the foundation of healthy communities, clean water, biodiversity, and sustainable development.

Forest Conservation

Supporting sustainable forest management, ecosystem restoration, and biodiversity conservation across Nepal.

Environmental Awareness

Organizing awareness programs, training, workshops, and educational campaigns for communities and youth.

Community Partnership

Working together with local communities, government agencies, and development partners for sustainable forestry.

Our Vision

A Nepal where forests thrive, biodiversity is protected, and communities prosper through sustainable natural resource management.

Our Mission

To strengthen forest conservation, promote responsible environmental practices, and empower communities through education, innovation, and collaboration.

Chairman's Message

Together We Can Build a Greener Future

Welcome to Forest Association Nepal Lumbini. Our forests are one of our country's greatest natural assets, supporting biodiversity, clean water, livelihoods, and future generations.

We believe conservation can only succeed through strong partnerships between communities, government agencies, educational institutions, and environmental organizations.

Let us continue protecting our forests while promoting sustainable development and responsible environmental stewardship.

Mr. Raghuvendra Pratap chaudhary
Chairman
