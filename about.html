Who We Are

Building a Sustainable Future for Nepal's Forests

Forest Association Nepal Lumbini is a non-profit organization dedicated to conserving forests, protecting biodiversity, and supporting sustainable forest management throughout Nepal.

We work closely with local communities, government agencies, educational institutions, and development partners to promote responsible forest conservation while improving livelihoods through environmentally sustainable practices.

Our programs focus on environmental awareness, capacity building, tree plantation, climate resilience, research, and community participation in forest management.

Our Journey

History of Forest Association Nepal Lumbini

Our organization has grown through years of dedication, community participation, and continuous commitment toward protecting Nepal's forests and natural resources.

Foundation

Forest Association Nepal Lumbini was established with the vision of promoting sustainable forest management and encouraging community participation in conservation activities.

Expansion

The association gradually expanded its programs across multiple districts by organizing awareness campaigns, tree plantation initiatives, and environmental education.

Today

Today, Forest Association Nepal Lumbini continues working with communities, educational institutions, government agencies, and development partners to conserve forests for future generations.

Our Vision

To create a greener, healthier, and environmentally sustainable Nepal where forests are protected, biodiversity is conserved, and local communities actively participate in responsible forest management.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable forestry, strengthen community participation, enhance environmental awareness, support scientific research, and encourage responsible utilization of natural resources while preserving ecosystems for future generations.
